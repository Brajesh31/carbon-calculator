# ✍️ Content and Claims Guide

Describe the project as an **educational carbon calculator prototype** or an **illustrative estimate**. Use plain language and explicit units. Avoid shaming users or implying individual behavior alone determines climate impact.

## Claims to avoid

Do not say "scientifically accurate", "verified footprint", "complete personal footprint", or "official national average" without evidence. Do not use CO₂e unless the methodology actually accounts for the relevant gases and conversion basis. Do not claim real-time emissions when static factors are used or verified reductions without a defensible baseline.

## Context required beside results

Explain the activity, input unit and period, factor source and vintage, geography, emissions boundary, gas basis, exclusions, uncertainty, and whether the estimate has been independently reviewed.

## Assets

Check reuse rights for images, icons, datasets, and factors. Prefer project-created diagrams. Preserve attribution where required.
