# 🌱 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Personal Carbon Calculator project welcomes contributors and users with varied backgrounds, experience, and perspectives. We aim to maintain a respectful, accessible, and constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Critique code, evidence, and proposals without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and engage in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information or private communications without permission.
- Be precise when discussing climate data, uncertainty, and methodology.
- Do not shame people for their reported footprint, income, location, diet, transport choices, or circumstances.
- Make discussions accessible to contributors with different technical backgrounds.
- Accept reasonable corrections and feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Demeaning language and repeated bad-faith derailment are also not welcome.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in this project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, Brajesh Kumar (@brajesh31), through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/brajesh31). Do not include sensitive details in public issues.

## Enforcement

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The maintainer will protect reporter privacy where possible and may request additional context. Depending on severity, actions may include a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Outcomes will be communicated when appropriate.
