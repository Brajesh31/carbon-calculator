# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Personal Carbon Calculator project welcomes contributions and discussion from people with varied backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to keep project spaces respectful, accessible, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Critique ideas, data, and code without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and respond to feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal data or private communications without permission.
- Be precise when discussing climate data and uncertainty; do not shame people for their reported footprint or circumstances.
- Keep discussion accessible to contributors with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussion or using demeaning language is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, Brajesh Kumar (@brajesh31), through the contact options on https://github.com/brajesh31. Do not include sensitive details in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and as promptly as practical, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may ask for more context. Depending on severity and circumstances, actions may include a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Outcomes will be communicated when appropriate.
